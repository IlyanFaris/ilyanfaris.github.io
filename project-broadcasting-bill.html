Platform governance and the Broadcasting Bill

Center for Digital Society (CfDS) UGM · 2025–2026 · Research Assistant

Summary

When a draft of Indonesia’s revised Broadcasting Bill (RUU Penyiaran) leaked in March 2024, it raised fears of a ban on investigative journalism and of the broadcasting regulator, KPI, extending its authority into digital spaces. This work looked at how that debate played out online and how content creators see the governance of the platforms they depend on.

What I did

I researched and wrote the CfDS report Dua Platform, Satu Kekhawatiran, which compares how netizens mobilised around the bill on X and on TikTok. I also led the enumeration team for a national survey of content creators’ perceptions of digital platform governance under the bill, which collected 585 responses.

Outputs
