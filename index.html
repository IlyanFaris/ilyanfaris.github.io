About Research Projects CV

I study the political economy of AI and digital platforms in the Global South, mostly through Indonesia.

I am a research assistant in the Partnership Division at the Center for Digital Society (CfDS), Universitas Gadjah Mada, and coordinate teaching assistants in UGM’s Department of International Relations, where I completed my bachelor’s degree in 2026.

My research asks who gains and who loses when AI and platforms reorganise work, speech and security. I have worked on algorithmic management in Indonesia’s gig economy, public debate over the Broadcasting Bill, deepfake regulation, and unequal access to AI for cyber defence. My thesis examined AI colonialism in creative platform labour through the case of Fotoyu and Indonesia’s freelance photographers.

Before CfDS, I was a research assistant at the Institute of International Studies (IIS) UGM and a research intern at AKATIGA Centre for Social Analysis. I also studied Intelligence Studies at Leiden University on an IISMA scholarship.
